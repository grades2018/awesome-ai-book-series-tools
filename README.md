## AI Writing Suites for Series

Long-form writing platforms with strong multi-book or continuity-aware workflows.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - End-to-end book workspace with series mode: plan and draft full manuscripts, preserve continuity via a story bible, and Manuscript Rewrite in the author’s voice across volumes.
- ⭐ [NovelCrafter](https://www.novelcrafter.com/) - Novel planning and prose workspace with Series Codex so characters and lore stay consistent book to book.
- ⭐ [Sudowrite](https://www.sudowrite.com/) - AI fiction suite with story bible / memory features useful when drafting sequels and shared casts.
- [Campfire](https://www.campfirewriting.com/) - Modular writing suite for plots, characters, manuscripts, and series-scale world data.
- [LivingWriter](https://www.livingwriter.com/) - Manuscript editor with AI helpers, outlines, and elements boards suited to multi-book projects.
- [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) - Cloud novel writing app with plot grids, goals, and series-friendly organization.
- [NovelPad](https://www.novelpad.co/) - Browser novel editor with plotting boards, goals, and AI-assisted drafting.
- [Squibler](https://www.squibler.io/) - Book writing platform with AI drafting, outlines, and manuscript organization.
- [NovelAI](https://novelai.net/) - Storytelling-focused AI for prose generation and fiction experimentation.

## Series Bibles & Continuity

Dedicated story-bible, cast, and continuity helpers for sequels and shared worlds.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Series story bible that carries characters, arcs, open threads, invented terms, and voice into the next volume.
- ⭐ [NovelCrafter](https://www.novelcrafter.com/) - Codex and Series Codex for searchable characters, places, lore, and progressions shared across books.
- [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) - Long-form project binder many series authors use for research folders and per-book manuscripts.
- [Obsidian](https://www.obsidian.md/) - Linked-note vault popular for series wikis, character sheets, and continuity graphs.
- [Notion](https://www.notion.so/) - Flexible databases and wikis for cast trackers, timeline tables, and series dashboards.

## Worldbuilding Wikis

Encyclopedia-style tools for settings, maps, and lore that span multiple books.

- [World Anvil](https://www.worldanvil.com/) - Worldbuilding wiki for settings, timelines, and character encyclopedias.
- [Kanka](https://kanka.io/) - Worldbuilding wiki for characters, locations, organizations, and calendars.
- [LegendKeeper](https://www.legendkeeper.com/) - Visual wiki/map notebook for series bibles and location lore.

## Plotting Across Volumes

Timeline and structure tools that scale past a single manuscript.

- ⭐ [Plottr](https://plottr.com/) - Visual timeline and template-based plotter widely used for series arcs and multi-book outlines.
- [Aeon Timeline](https://www.aeontimeline.com/) - Professional timeline software for tracking events, characters, and chronology across volumes.
- [One Stop for Writers](https://onestopforwriters.com/) - Thesaurus sets, character builders, and story maps helpful for emotional arcs across a series.

## Editing & Voice Consistency

Tools that help keep prose voice and continuity clean as the series grows.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Manuscript Rewrite for polishing or voice-shifting drafts while preserving what already works in prior books.
- [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/) - In-depth style and manuscript reports for catching drift across long projects.
- [AutoCrit](https://www.autocrit.com/) - Fiction-focused manuscript critique with genre-aware analysis options.
- [Marlowe (Authors.ai)](https://authors.ai/marlowe/) - Analytical AI manuscript feedback on plot, pacing, and character for fiction.

## Formatting & Export

Export and interior tools for shipping each volume in a series catalog.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Reader/editor screen for formatting and line edits, with export to EPUB, Word, and Markdown.
- [Atticus](https://www.atticus.io/) - Cross-platform book formatter for consistent ebook and print interiors across a series.
- [Vellum](https://vellum.pub/) - Mac book formatting software popular for matching series branding in print and ebook.
- [Reedsy Studio](https://reedsy.com/studio) - Free writing and formatting studio plus access to professional editors.

## Covers & Series Branding

Visual tools for matching covers and titles across a series spine.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Cover and title engine for unique covers and title ideas matched to each manuscript while supporting series look-and-feel.
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) - Design tool with AI helpers for series templates, boxed-set graphics, and promo assets.
- [BookBrush](https://bookbrush.com/) - Author-focused graphics studio for 3D covers, mockups, and series promo images.

## General AI Assistants

General-purpose models series writers use for continuity checks, recaps, and brainstorming.

- [Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/claude) - Long-context AI assistant strong at series recaps, continuity questions, and chapter feedback.
- [ChatGPT](https://openai.com/chatgpt/overview) - General AI chatbot for brainstorming arcs, character sheets, and sequel outlines.
- [Google Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) - Multimodal assistant for research, outlines, and drafting support.
